# Disclaimer

This repository exists solely to practise data engineering, Python, probabilistic
modelling and machine-learning workflows. It is not financial, racing or betting advice
and does not recommend wagering on any horse.

The projections are experimental and may be wrong. They use a pre-final-field market
snapshot; odds, acceptances, veterinary status, barriers, jockeys and track conditions
may change. The educational model has not been validated as a profitable system and
must not be used to make financial decisions.

The phrase “winner selection” identifies the highest probability in the model output.
It does not mean that the recorded market price offers positive expected value. The
project explicitly reports a no-bet result when the available price is below model fair
odds plus the stated safety margin.

If gambling is causing concern in Australia, contact
[Gambling Help Online](https://www.gamblinghelponline.org.au/) or call 1800 858 858.
Support is free, confidential and available 24 hours a day.
